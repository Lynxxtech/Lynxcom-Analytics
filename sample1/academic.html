Client explanation

Academic reporting is separate from business reporting.

The same data-entry portal captures results, but this dashboard gives the proprietor and head teacher a focused view of learning outcomes without mixing it with fees, expenses and debtors.

  1. Teachers or admin enter results through the Result / Assessment form.
  2. The academic dashboard tracks class, subject, teacher and term performance.
  3. Weak subjects and at-risk students become action items for management.
